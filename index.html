About the Project

The problem

Tonnes of plastic enter the ocean every year. Lost fishing nets and plastic waste harm fish, turtles and seabirds. At the same time, many coastal fishing families have a low and uncertain income.

Our solution

This website lets fishermen earn a bounty for every kilogram of plastic they recover. Each weigh-in is recorded with the fisherman's name, boat ID, plastic weight and GPS position, so the payout is transparent and the cleaned areas can be tracked.
